Demonstration of the period-4 flash-induced oxygen evolution discovered by Pierre Joliot and Bessel Kok.

Kok Cycle Mechanism: $2\text{H}_2\text{O} \xrightarrow{4 h\nu} \text{O}_2 + 4\text{H}^+_{\text{lumen}} + 4e^-$ through states $S_0 \xrightarrow{h\nu} S_1 \xrightarrow{h\nu} S_2 \xrightarrow{h\nu} S_3 \xrightarrow{h\nu} [S_4] \to S_0 + \text{O}_2$

💡 Dark Adaptation ($S_1$ Rest State)

In dark-adapted chloroplasts, the $S_1$ state is thermodynamically stable ($S_1 \approx 75\%$, $S_0 \approx 25\%$). As a result, the maximum $\text{O}_2$ yield occurs exactly on the 3rd laser flash, and subsequent peaks appear every 4 flashes (flash 7, 11, 15...).

🧪 Manganese Oxidation States

The Mn₄ cluster transitions through higher oxidation numbers ($\text{Mn}^{3+} \to \text{Mn}^{4+}$). At $S_3$, an active tyrosine radical ($\text{Y}_Z^\bullet$) coordinates with the distorted Mn4-O5 bridge to trigger $\text{O=O}$ bond coupling in transient $[S_4]$.

📉 Misses & Double Hits Dampening

The flash yield oscillation slowly dampens due to photochemical "misses" (~10% probability an active center fails to advance) and rare "double hits" (~5% probability of absorbing two excitons in one flash).

Phase 1: Carbon Fixation

RuBisCO carboxylates 3 molecules of Ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate ($\text{C}_5$) to form a transient $\text{C}_6$ intermediate that hydrolyzes into 6 molecules of 3-Phosphoglycerate ($\text{3-PGA}$).

Phase 2: Phosphorylation & Reduction

6 Phosphoglycerate kinases consume 6 ATP to make 1,3-bisphosphoglycerate, which 6 G3P dehydrogenases reduce using 6 NADPH to yield 6 Triose Phosphates ($\text{G3P}$). 1 net $\text{G3P}$ is exported to sucrose/starch synthesis.

Phase 3: RuBP Regeneration

The remaining 5 $\text{G3P}$ ($\text{C}_{15}$) undergo complex rearrangements via transketolase, aldolase, and SBPase, then are phosphorylated by Phosphoribulokinase (consuming 3 ATP) to regenerate 3 RuBP.

🌿 Chlorophyll a (Soret & Qy Bands)

Primary reaction center chromophore. Peaks sharply in the blue Soret region ($430\text{ nm}$) and the red $Q_y$ region ($662\text{ nm}$). Exhibits the infamous "green window" of low absorption between $500\text{--}600\text{ nm}$.

🌱 Chlorophyll b (Accessory Antenna)

Features a formyl group ($-\text{CHO}$) in place of methyl ($-\text{CH}_3$), shifting absorption peaks to $453\text{ nm}$ and $642\text{ nm}$, thereby broadening solar photon capture for LHCII.

🥕 Carotenoids (Photoprotection & NPQ)

Lutein, $\beta$-carotene, and zeaxanthin absorb strongly between $400\text{--}500\text{ nm}$. Under excess photon flux, the xanthophyll cycle safely dissipates triplet chlorophyll states as thermal heat via Non-Photochemical Quenching (NPQ).
